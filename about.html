Patient-first dentistry, since 2005

The dental practice of the Prudent International Health Clinic group: one dedicated dental home in Viman Nagar, caring for patients from across Pune.

Our story

Prevention and plain-language explanations are the base of everything.

Every visit starts with a thorough examination. We check the health of your teeth and gums, take X-rays only when needed, and focus on cleanings, sealants and fluoride that prevent disease.

We care about the comfort of your treatment as much as the look of your smile. Dr. Bansal and the team are friendly and gentle, and that personal approach is what makes the practice feel like family.

Our mission

“To provide the best and most exceptional dental health care to our patients with genuine personal attention and care, while observing every necessary medical and sterilisation precaution.”

Sterilisation & safety

Infection control shapes how we work

One staff member handles general cleaning, kept separate from anyone working with clinical instruments. Standards are reviewed by Dr. Bansal.

Fresh protection, every patient

Gloves, mask, eye protection and a head cap for each appointment.

Autoclaved instruments

Cleaned, sealed in pouches, sterilised and stored in a UV chamber until use.

Deep-cleaned twice daily

Chair and surfaces disinfected and barrier-covered between patients; single-use items never reused.
