Costs & safety

There is no fixed online price, because cost depends on your diagnosis and the materials used. After an examination you get a clear, written, itemised estimate before anything starts, so you are never committed to work you have not agreed to.

Yes. EMI is available for larger treatments such as implants, smile makeovers and braces, including no-cost EMI on eligible plans. Payment options are explained on top of your written estimate, never instead of it.

Most Indian health policies exclude routine dental work; accident-related and hospitalisation cases are the usual exceptions. Some corporate and newer retail policies include dental OPD add-ons, so check your specific policy wording.

Instruments are autoclaved, single-use items are never reused, surfaces are disinfected and barrier-covered between patients, and biomedical waste is handled properly. The clinic is deep-cleaned twice daily.

Emergencies

Call the clinic and describe the problem rather than booking online, so you can be prioritised. We are open all 7 days, 9 AM to 9 PM (5 PM on Sundays). We are not a 24-hour clinic.

For a knocked-out adult tooth, keep it moist in milk and get to a dentist within about an hour for the best chance of saving it. Call us on the way.

For uncontrolled bleeding, spreading facial swelling, or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to a hospital emergency department straight away.
